Pricing FAQ

Common questions.

How does the free pilot work?

Pick one or two classes, run them on ClassIQ for a full term at no cost. Most schools roll out to the rest of the school once the teachers in the pilot say so.

Do you charge teachers or staff separately?

No. Pricing is purely per-student. Teachers, parents, principals, admins and support staff are all included. You get access to entire family with one student rate.

What about the modules listed as "coming soon"?

Exams & analytics, biometric bus tracking and online fee payments are in active development. Schools on the School plan get them in early-access at no extra cost as they ship.

Can we move our data out if we leave?

Yes. You can export student records, communication archive and notices at any time. We believe your school's data belongs to you.

Is ClassIQ compliant with Indian regulations?

Yes. ClassIQ is designed to comply with India's DPDP Act, including parental consent for student data. Hosted in Indian data centres.
