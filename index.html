The problem with school today

Most schools are run on group chats. That has a cost.

P · 01

Communication chaos

  • Teacher messages buried in noisy class groups
  • Parents miss critical circulars
  • Repeated calls increase teacher workload
P · 02

No school control

  • Chats happen on unmanaged third-party apps
  • Personal phone numbers exposed
  • No visibility into who saw what
P · 03

Parent overload

  • Too many unrelated messages drown the important ones
  • Homework and notices vanish in chat history
  • Hard to tell official from casual
P · 04

Admin inefficiency

  • Groups rebuilt by hand every academic year
  • Attendance, homework, notices in separate places
  • Student info scattered across systems

For administrators

  • Centralized communication oversight across all classes
  • Acknowledgement tracking for every official notice

  • Automatic academic-year rollover and class promotion

  • Long-term student records with continuity across years
For teachers

Your day, without the noise.

Send what matters, get heard, and stop being on call after hours. Your personal number stays personal.

  • Teacher-priority messages cut through chatter
