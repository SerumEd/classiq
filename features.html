F · 01 · Communication

Chats for classes, subjects and staff.

A clean, school-managed alternative to WhatsApp groups. Class channels, subject-specific groups and a private staff room — all moderated, all carrying forward into the next academic year.

  • Class-wise channels with the homeroom teacher at the helm
  • Subject-specific groups for Maths, Science and beyond
  • Private staff group chats for school operations
  • Auto-rollover — no recreating groups every June
  • Teacher moderation tools — pin, mute, archive

F · 02 · Community

The school's own community feed.

A social-style feed for the events, achievements and small moments that make a school feel like a school — without it ever becoming a chat group.

  • Staff and admin can post; parents and students can react
  • Native reactions — Like, Love, Proud, Insightful, Amazing
  • Tag students in posts to celebrate them
  • Photos and short videos supported
  • Discoverable archive — past years stay browseable
F · 03 · Announcements

Notice board, organized for the long term.

Official circulars stay pinned and discoverable. Whoever needed to see it can come back and find it — even six months later.

  • Pin official circulars and announcements
  • Target by class, grade, section or whole school
  • Attach PDFs, forms, consent slips
  • Persistent searchable archive
  • Send instant notifications when a notice goes out

F · 04 · Events

Events & competitions, end-to-end.

From school day to the annual day, classIQ runs the entire event lifecycle — planning, media, winners and certificates — without spreadsheets.

  • Create events with date, location, category and applicable grades
  • Track participants per event
  • Announce winners and runners-up with rank
  • Attach photos and videos to every event
  • Generate winner & participant certificates automatically
F · 05 · Daily workflow

Attendance, in two taps.

Mark the daily roll call in seconds. Absent students' parents get notified automatically — and your records build over the year.

  • Tap-to-mark present / absent / late
  • Parent notifications on absence
  • Per-student attendance history across years
  • Class- and student-level reports
  • Section-wise summaries for the principal

F · 06 · Households

One phone, multiple logins. No logout required.

A household with two siblings and two parents shares one phone for half the day. classIQ handles this natively — switch between profiles in a tap.

  • Multiple profile logins on a single device
  • Switch between parent / mother / child profiles instantly
  • Per-profile notifications and data
  • Designed for real Indian households
F · 07 · Records

The complete student profile.

Every student gets a full profile — details, family, contacts, mentions in posts and event wins — all in one place and carried forward year after year.

  • Student details — admission no, DOB, class, blood group
  • Parent information — father and mother on file
  • Contact information for student and parents
  • Mentioned posts from the school feed
  • Event wins and participations across years
