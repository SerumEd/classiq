Who we are

We're building the operating system Indian schools deserve.

classIQ started in a school staff-room. A small team of teachers, parents and product builders watched a generation of educators stitch their jobs together from WhatsApp, paper diaries and printed circulars — and built the alternative.

What we believe

Our principles.

Four ideas that shape every decision we make about classIQ.

P · 01

Schools first, software second

Every feature has to survive a Monday morning in a real classroom. If a feature looks great in a demo but slows down a class teacher with 38 students, it doesn't ship.

P · 02

Quiet by default

The right number of notifications is the smallest one. Parents shouldn't have to mute us to live their day. Teachers shouldn't have to answer calls after hours.

P · 03

The school owns the graph

The relationship between teacher and parent belongs to the school, not to a third-party chat app. classIQ is structured so a school's communication life is auditable, exportable and entirely its own.

P · 04

Built for India, built to last

2G-friendly. Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Marathi and Telugu language packs. DPDP-compliant. Hosted in Indian data centres. classIQ is built to outlast a single trend.
