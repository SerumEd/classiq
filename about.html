How it started

We started building in 2025. We're shipping in 2026.

After twenty years at companies that obsessed over millisecond latencies for billions of users, we turned our attention to a problem that mattered closer to home: how Indian schools actually communicate. classIQ is the operating system we wished existed when our kids started school — engineered with the discipline of platforms that have never been allowed to fail.
